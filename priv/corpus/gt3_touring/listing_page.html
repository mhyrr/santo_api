3,600-Mile Linden Green 2018 Porsche 911 GT3 Touring 6-Speed

USA
N No Reserve

This 2018 Porsche 911 GT3 Touring has 3,600 miles and was optioned from the factory with a paint-to-sample Linden Green exterior finish in addition to a six-speed manual transaxle, which is paired with a 4.0-liter flat-six. The car is further equipped with black leather and cloth upholstery, the Sport Chrono Package, 20″ center-lock alloy wheels, Porsche Ceramic Composite Brakes, LED headlights with PDLS, the Touring Package in Black, a front-axle lift system, a back-up camera, 18-way adaptive Sport Plus seats, PCM with navigation, a Bose sound system, and Porsche Torque Vectoring. This 991.2 GT3 Touring was purchased by the owner in 2020 and is now offered on dealer consignment at no reserve with a window sticker, the owner’s manual, service records, a clean Carfax report, and a clean Arizona title.

The car is finished in paint-to-sample Linden Green and was optioned with the Touring Package in Black, which added black window trim and rear Porsche lettering as well as a revised decklid with a mesh grille and a retractable rear spoiler in place of the raised wing found on the standard GT3. Additional exterior details include LED headlights with PDLS, a back-up camera, auto-dimming mirrors, window tint, and LED taillights.

Black-finished 20″ center-lock alloy wheels are mounted with Michelin tires showing 2018 date codes. Braking is handled by yellow Porsche-branded calipers over ceramic-composite rotors, and the car is equipped with a front-axle lift system. A brake fluid flush was performed in preparation for the sale.

The 18-way adaptive sport seats are trimmed in black leather and cloth with Porsche crests on the headrests, while Porsche-branded floor mats line the footwells. Appointments include a fire extinguisher, Porsche Communication Management with navigation, automatic climate control, a Bose sound system, the Light Design Package, and cruise control.

The three-spoke steering wheel frames a central GT3-branded tachometer as well as a 225-mph speedometer, an LCD screen, and gauges for oil pressure, oil temperature, coolant temperature, and fuel level. The optional Sport Chrono Package added a stopwatch atop the dashboard. The digital odometer indicates 3,600 miles, approximately 2,300 of which were added under current ownership.

The 4.0-liter flat-six was factory rated at 500 horsepower and 339 lb-ft of torque. The car was optioned with an extended range 23.7-gallon fuel tank. Work in preparation for the sale included changing the oil in addition to replacing the battery, drive belt, trunk and engine cover struts, air filters, and spark plugs. Results of a DME report are provided in the gallery.

Power is sent to the rear wheels through a six-speed manual transaxle with Porsche Torque Vectoring. Additional photos of the underside are provided in the gallery.

The window sticker lists initial delivery to Porsche Plano in Texas, along with factory options and a total price of $186,380.

The Carfax report is free of accidents or other reported damage.
